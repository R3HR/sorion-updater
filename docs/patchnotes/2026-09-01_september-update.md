# SORION Update — September 1st

A big batch of improvements went live over the last days:

**🔍 All-new market filters**
- Leagues now follow Sorare's own structure: named competitions, **Champion** and **Contender** groups (one click filters the whole group), Under 23 and Rest of the World
- Club search is pre-filtered by your selected league
- New filters: **nationality**, **age**, **price range** and **player tier** — all combinable

**⭐ Player tiers everywhere**
The Tier column now shows each player's rating as stars (GOAT ★★★★★ → DNP ★), same as you know it from Sorare.

**💼 Portfolio upgrades**
- The full market filter set is now available in your portfolio too
- **NET values already include Sorare's new €0.10 minimum sale fee** for cards under €2 — announced this week, and roughly every second card is affected. Break-even prices are adjusted accordingly.
- Click your name (top right) to open your new **profile overview** — portfolio first, **gameweek history coming soon**

**🎯 Accuracy, out in the open**
Our FMV price model got a major precision upgrade this week. We measure every estimate against real sales and publish the numbers daily — including a live head-to-head against the floor price: [sorion.pro/accuracy](https://sorion.pro/accuracy.html)

**🩹 Fixes**
- English leagues no longer mix up Premier League and Championship players
- League names follow Sorare's renames (MLS → Major League Soccer etc.)
- Faster, more reliable page loads on mobile
