# SORION Update: who actually brings the money home

New on the **Leaderboards** page, right below the main table: **Top earners**. For every competition you can now see every player who earned something this season, in cash or in essence. The top 25 come first, the full list is one click away, with a search box to find your own players.

**◆ How it works**
Every winning lineup's prize is split across its cards by their **share of the points**. A player who scored 80 of the lineup's 400 points gets credited with 20 %. Added up over every gameweek of the season, that tells you which cards actually carry lineups into the money, not just which ones score well on paper.

Pick a competition in the dropdown, or simply click one in the main table and the page jumps there. Switch between **Cash** and **Essence**, and use the rarity buttons at the top as before. Next to each player you see the club he scored those points for, how many winning lineups he was in and over how many gameweeks.

**✦ Cash and essence**
Cash counts every rank that was paid in USD. Essence counts every rank that received essence, which in the big competitions means tens of thousands of lineups per gameweek. Unique competitions pay their essence as Super Rare essence, so that is what you see there.

The **essence** rankings are free for everyone. The **cash** rankings are part of Pro, with **English League Players** open to everyone as a free sample.

**▦ Also new since the last update**
• **Cheapest 20 % and cheapest winner**: two new columns showing how cheap a squad that got paid can be. One in five winning lineups cost the first number or less, the second is the single cheapest one all season.
• **MLS, J1 League and K League 1** are in the table. They keep playing through the European winter and are by far the cheapest essence entries on the page.
• The page now **updates itself every Tuesday and Friday evening**, right after Sorare pays out the rewards.
