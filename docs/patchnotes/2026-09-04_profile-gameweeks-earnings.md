# SORION Update: your profile, your gameweeks, your earnings

Click your name (top right) to open your new **profile overview**.

**▦ Gameweek History (new)**
Every gameweek you have played, back to your first: each lineup with rank, percentile and score, every player with their weighted score, and every reward. Bench and substituted cards are clearly marked, so only cards that actually counted are listed as scoring.

**✦ Earnings per card**
A lineup's reward is split across its scoring cards **by the share of points each contributed**. So every card now shows what it really earned you:

> €2.72 prize money · 735 limited essence (≈€3.40) · 1,224 Limited XP

Money, XP and essence stay separate, because they are not the same thing. The € next to essence is an **estimate** from craft results logged in [CraftLog](https://craftlog.pro). It rests on 68 crafts so far (rare: just a handful), so take it as a first indication. Every craft logged there sharpens it for everyone, and logging will soon require an account with verified entries, so the rate stays trustworthy.

New filters: show only cards that **earned something**, or sort by **Top Earners**. Own a player twice? Each card counts on its own.

**💼 Portfolio**: filters now live in a sidebar, grouped by what they do, and card details are bigger and easier to read.

Works for any manager, no login needed: `sorion.pro/gameweeks.html?manager=<slug>`
