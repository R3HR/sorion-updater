# Valuation beta: v3.7 is live

When a card keeps selling below our value, our number used to lag behind. From now on the value follows a running trend faster, in both directions. Cards that are sliding come down sooner, cards on the way up catch up sooner.

How much better: on cards that keep falling our estimate is about **30% more accurate**, on cards that keep rising about **15%**. Across all cards the gain is small, roughly **2%**, because most cards are not trending in one direction. We also no longer sit systematically too high on falling cards. The trade-off is that values move a little more from day to day.

This is a beta: if a card looks off to you, tell us in #feedback. The last report led straight to this change. 🙏
