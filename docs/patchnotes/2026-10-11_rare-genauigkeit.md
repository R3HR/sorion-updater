# Rare values just got much more accurate

We measured our Rare values against real manager sales and they were not good enough: the typical miss was about 25%, and we sat systematically too high. For Rare we now also take the live cheapest offer into account, weighted by how thinly each card trades.

Typical miss after the change: Rare In-Season about 16%, Rare Classic about 22%, Super Rare about 26%. Limited stays as it was, it was already our most accurate segment.

New values show up from tomorrow morning. The 7 day change chip stays empty for a week while the new numbers build up, that is intentional.

Spotted a card that still looks wrong? Tell us in #feedback, that is exactly how this one got found. 🙏
