# SORION Update: Leaderboards, or what it actually takes to get paid

New page in the top menu: **Leaderboards**. For every closed gameweek this season we read each competition's reward tiers and the exact score of the **last manager who still got paid**. Averaged over the season, that is the number you can plan a lineup against.

**▦ The score you need**
Per competition and rarity: how many SO5 points it took for **essence**, how deep the paid ranks reach, the winning score, and how many lineups you were up against. The small range behind each number shows how much it swings from week to week. Some competitions are steady, others move by 100 points.

**✦ What a winning squad costs**
The other half of the question. We read the actual lineups of managers who finished in the money, price all five cards at today's FMV and take the median. So you can see what it costs to assemble a squad that gets paid, not what its owner paid two years ago.

The spread is bigger than you might expect. For Limited, essence starts around **€27** in Contender and **€36** in Under 23, while LALIGA asks about **€120** for the same result. Same effort, very different entry ticket.

Two things to keep in mind. All Star and Under 23 are played with seven cards, everything else with five, so compare within a competition. And only fully priced lineups count, so a squad with one unpriced card never drags the number down.
